# Brand Kit — Premium Bamboo-Viscose/Cotton Towels (AU DTC)

**Positioning band:** Mainstream eco, AU$39–60 bath-towel tier. Competes on softness, plushness (600–700 GSM), quick-dry, considered design, honest labelling. Not luxury, not cheap-and-cheerful.

**Compliance posture (read first):** Product is bamboo *viscose* (rayon), chemically processed, Made in Thailand. Every name, tagline and line of copy below has been written to stay clear of ACCC greenwashing exposure. We never say "100% bamboo", "naturally antibacterial", "hypoallergenic", "chemical-free", "UV protection", "eco/sustainable" unqualified, or anything implying Australian manufacture. The defensible claim is **"made with bamboo viscose"** plus sensory/performance attributes (softness, weight, drape, quick-dry).

---

## 1. Name candidates

### Candidate A — **Saltmist**
- **Why it works:** Coastal-Australian without being a postcard cliché. Evokes the soft, cool, after-the-swim feeling — directly tied to towels and to the sensory promise. One ownable word, easy .com.au.
- **Vibe:** Calm, coastal, contemporary. Linen-light, salt-air, early morning.
- **Risk check:** Clean. Makes zero material/origin/health claim. No greenwashing surface.

### Candidate B — **Hush & Co** (wordmark: **Hush.**)
- **Why it works:** "Hush" sells the *feel* and the *calm ritual* — softness as quietness. Short, premium, voice-driven. Sits comfortably beside Aesop/Bed Threads tonal neighbours.
- **Vibe:** Quiet luxury-adjacent, sensory, considered. Bathroom-as-sanctuary.
- **Risk check:** Clean. Pure emotion, no claim. Slight risk of "Hush" being crowded across categories — defensible because we own the homewares lane and the full lockup "Hush & Co".

### Candidate C — **Dunelight**
- **Why it works:** "Dune" pulls the warm, sandy, coastal-Australian palette; "light" nods to quick-dry and to lightness of feel and mood. Distinctive coined compound, unlikely to clash with incumbents.
- **Vibe:** Warm, sunlit, natural-neutral. Bondi-to-Byron without saying it.
- **Risk check:** Clean. "Light" reads as airiness/quick-dry, not a health or eco claim.

### Candidate D — **Nara Home**
- **Why it works:** "Nara" is soft, warm, phonetically gentle, and reads as a quietly premium label name (think Bed Threads, In Bed). "Home" widens beyond towels for range extension into robes, sheets, bath mats.
- **Vibe:** Modern homewares house. Editorial, neutral, grown-up.
- **Risk check:** Clean — but "Nara" is also a Japanese city/place and a moderately used brand token; check trademark in AU class 24/35 before committing. Slightly less ownable than A–C.

### RECOMMENDED — **Saltmist**

It is the most ownable single-word name, it is unmistakably Australian-coastal without leaning on the green/bamboo cliché that creates legal risk, and it ties straight back to the product's core sensory moment (the soft towel after water). It carries a premium-but-approachable tone that fits the AU$39–60 band exactly — warmer than a luxury monogram brand, more considered than a value brand. It extends cleanly to a range (Saltmist Bath Sheet, Saltmist Robe) and gives art direction a built-in mood: salt air, soft light, cool neutrals. Crucially it makes no implied claim of any kind, so the brand surface itself is greenwash-proof.

## 2. The Saltmist brand

**Positioning statement (one line):**
Saltmist makes plush, quick-drying bath towels made with bamboo viscose and cotton — considered design and honest labelling, for the part of the day that's just yours.

**Primary tagline:**
**Softness, settled.**

**Alternate taglines:**
- *The good part of the day.*
- *Made soft. Made plain about it.* (leans into honest-labelling positioning)

**Brand story (~100 words, claims-safe):**

Saltmist started with a simple, unglamorous test: the towel you actually reach for. Not the showroom one — the everyday one that feels plush, dries fast, and still looks good a year in. Our towels are woven from a bamboo-viscose and cotton blend at 600–700 GSM, so they're dense and soft underhand but quick to dry on the rail. They're made in Thailand by a mill we work with directly, and we label them plainly — what they're made of, how they wash, nothing dressed up. No miracle claims. Just a genuinely good towel, and a calmer few minutes in your day.

**Brand voice — 3 principles:**

1. **Plain-spoken, not flat.** We describe the feel honestly and let the product carry it. Warm and human, never breathless. If we can't substantiate it, we don't say it.
2. **Sensory over technical.** Lead with how it feels — plush, dense, soft, quick to dry — then back it with the spec (GSM, blend) for people who want it. Specs build trust; feeling sells.
3. **Calm and unhurried.** Short sentences. Room to breathe. The brand is the quiet bit of the day; the copy should feel like that too.

**Do / Don't word list:**

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| made with bamboo viscose | 100% bamboo / pure bamboo |
| soft, plush, dense, cushioned | naturally antibacterial / odour-fighting |
| quick-dry, dries fast, airy | hypoallergenic |
| 600–700 GSM, weighty, substantial | eco / sustainable / green (unqualified) |
| considered, honest, plainly labelled | chemical-free / all-natural / non-toxic |
| made in Thailand (stated plainly) | UV protection / sun-safe |
| everyday ritual, the quiet minutes | Aussie-made / designed-and-made-here |
| sensory, warm, calm, settled | luxury, opulent, indulgent excess |

> Note on "designed in Australia": only usable if the design work is genuinely done in AU and it is never paired in a way that implies *manufacture* here. Safest default in copy is "Made in Thailand. Designed for here."

## 3. Visual identity direction

**Colour palette (warm, calm, contemporary Australian — deliberately not green):**

| Role | Name | HEX |
|---|---|---|
| Primary background / paper | Bone | `#F4EFE6` |
| Ink / primary text | Driftwood Ink | `#2E2A24` |
| Brand accent (warm) | Terracotta Wash | `#C8754B` |
| Secondary neutral | Warm Sand | `#D8C7AE` |
| Cool support (the "mist") | Sea Mist | `#8FA3A0` |
| Deep anchor / footers | Kelp | `#3F4A45` |

The palette is built around warm bone-and-sand neutrals with a single warm terracotta accent for CTAs and brand moments, and a muted sage-grey ("Sea Mist") as the only cool tone — it nods to coast and softness without dropping into the predictable eco-green. Kelp gives a near-black with warmth for footers and contrast. Terracotta on Bone is the signature pairing.

**Typography pairing (both free, Google Fonts):**

- **Display / headings: Fraunces.** A warm, soft-serif with gentle "old-style" character and an optical/soft setting — premium and editorial without being stiff or luxury-cold. Use in the higher weights (Black/SemiBold) for hero lines, set generously with tight-ish tracking.
- **Body / UI: Inter.** Clean, neutral, exceptionally legible at all sizes; carries product specs, PDP copy and checkout cleanly. Pairs with Fraunces by contrast — character up top, calm and functional underneath.

Pairing rule: Fraunces for emotion (hero, section titles, taglines), Inter for everything functional (body, specs, nav, buttons, legal). Never set body in Fraunces.

**Logo concept:**

Wordmark-led. "Saltmist" set in Fraunces SemiBold, lowercase or sentence-case, with slightly open letter-spacing to feel airy. Optional brand mark: a single soft, rounded horizontal stroke or a minimal abstract "wave/horizon line" that can sit above the wordmark or work as a standalone favicon/app icon. Keep it monoline and quiet — no literal bamboo leaf, no droplet, no leaf-in-a-circle eco trope (those also visually over-claim). Primary lockup: Driftwood Ink on Bone. Reverse: Bone on Kelp. Accent lockup (sparingly): Terracotta wordmark on Bone.

**Art direction:**

- **Photography:** Natural, soft, directional morning light; warm shadows. Towels shot in real, lived-in spaces — folded on a timber rail, draped over a chair, stacked on a stone or terracotta-tiled surface. Close-up texture/macro shots to sell plushness and weave density (the spec, made visible). Skin and water present but understated — the after-swim, post-shower calm, not a hard fitness or spa cliché.
- **Texture & materials:** Bone paper, raw timber, unglazed terracotta, linen, sandstone, sea-glass tones. Tactile, matte, never glossy.
- **Mood:** Quiet, warm, unhurried, grown-up coastal. Editorial homewares (Bed Threads / In Bed / Cultiver tonal neighbourhood) but warmer and more accessible. Lots of negative space; the product and the light do the work.
- **Composition:** Generous white(bone) space, asymmetric layouts, large Fraunces headlines over calm photography, specs in small clean Inter.

## 4. Product naming convention

**Structure:** `Saltmist [Format]` for core SKUs; colour as the variant, not part of the product name. Keep format names plain and category-standard so search and comprehension stay easy — the brand carries the premium tone, the format name stays honest.

| Item | Product name | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Bath towel | **Saltmist Bath Towel** | Core hero SKU |
| Bath sheet (larger) | **Saltmist Bath Sheet** | Larger format, same line |
| Hand towel | **Saltmist Hand Towel** | |
| Face washer | **Saltmist Face Cloth** | |
| Bath mat | **Saltmist Bath Mat** | Range extension |
| Bundle (set) | **Saltmist Bathroom Set** | e.g. 2 bath towels + 2 hand towels + 2 face cloths |
| Curated bundle | **The Saltmist Bundle** | Marketing-led hero set, lead offer |

**Colourways** (named to match the palette, never as a separate product): Bone, Warm Sand, Terracotta, Sea Mist, Kelp. Display as "Saltmist Bath Towel — Sea Mist".

**Spec block convention (always paired, for honesty + trust):**
> 600–700 GSM · Bamboo viscose / cotton blend · Quick-dry · Made in Thailand

Keep this exact factual block on every PDP. It does the compliance heavy lifting: states the real fibre (viscose, not "bamboo"), the real origin, and the substantiated performance attributes — no claim beyond what's true.
